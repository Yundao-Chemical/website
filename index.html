上海耘稻农业化工有限公司

Shanghai Yundao Agricultural Chemical Co., Ltd.

Shanghai Yundao Agricultural Chemical Co., Ltd. is a China-based foreign trade company focused on agricultural chemicals. Our product portfolio covers herbicides, insecticides, fungicides and fertilizers, connecting product sourcing with the needs of international customers.

Our brand brings together agriculture and global connections: nature, science, cultivation and harvest, alongside international trade, communication and professionalism. The YD logo incorporates a grain-inspired detail, reflecting this agricultural focus.

The company holds a Chinese hazardous chemicals operating license for operations without storage facilities. Specific product scope and destination requirements are confirmed for each inquiry.
